04

Review the build

The most important screen in the product. One artifact shown three ways, rather than three artifacts that have to be kept in agreement. The chips in the script are not a rendering of a variable name, they are the reference itself, pointing at a field by id. That is why the middle panel has no Add button: the fields your list must supply are exactly the chips the script uses, so you manage them by editing the script. Nobody types curly braces anywhere in this product. The extraction schema stays editable through testing and freezes only at launch, so this screen and screen 06 are one loop rather than two steps.

These are three views of one thing, not three things. The fields your list must supply are simply the chips used in the script, so you add and remove them by editing the script itself. What gets extracted is a separate list you edit directly. Rename anything freely: names live in one place, so nothing else needs updating.
What gets extracted stays editable until you launch. Keep changing the schema and re-testing for as long as you like — each test call is billed at the normal rate. The moment this agent starts dialling, the schema freezes for the life of the run and past calls cannot be re-scored. To change it after that, duplicate the agent.

THE ONE ARTIFACT

The call script

Role. You are calling on behalf of Sylvastar Residences. You are warm, efficient, and you never oversell. Keep the whole call under two minutes.

Opening. Greet first_name by name, say you are calling from Sylvastar, and state the purpose in one sentence. Nigerian consumer rules require you to identify yourself and your purpose before anything else.

Confirm. Remind them their site visit is booked for visit_date at visit_time. Ask whether they are still planning to attend.

If they cannot make it. Ask what changed, without pressing. Offer to reschedule and capture their preferred window.

Qualify. Whether or not they are attending, try to learn the budget range they are working with and when they are hoping to move in. Ask once. Do not push if they deflect.

Boundaries. If asked about pricing specifics, say a consultant will follow up with the full breakdown. Never quote a figure. Never promise availability.

Close. Thank them by name and end the call.

When it reaches voicemail

On this list, roughly 17% of connects hit voicemail. Leaving a message costs about 18 seconds each, around $1.60 per 100 calls.

06

Test call

Not a checkpoint, a loop. Edit the schema, call again, hear what changed. When something sounds wrong you describe it in plain words and it is rewritten and re-dialled without leaving the screen. Test values are typed stand-ins; checking the contact list against the script is the import screen’s job, and free. Each round is a real billed call, which is the point: Telnyx has no way to re-score a transcript, so the only honest test of a schema is the same path production will take. This loop closes the moment the agent launches.
